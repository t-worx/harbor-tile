West Palm Beach floors, and what they go through

This city asks a lot of a floor. Here is what we see most often.

Historic tile in the older districts

Many homes in El Cid, Flamingo Park, Grandview Heights and Northwood date from the 1920s and still have original cement, terracotta or terrazzo floors. Porous tile and wide grout lines hold onto soil, so the clean has to suit the surface.

Polished stone in waterfront condos

Light marble and porcelain along Flagler Drive show every traffic path, and pale grout is the first thing to darken.

Humidity, sand and salt air

South Florida humidity and the sand that comes home from the beach work their way into grout and carpet pile. Regular deep cleaning keeps both from going grey.

Pets, and life at home

Dogs, kids and guests leave spots and odors in carpet and upholstery that everyday vacuuming cannot reach.
