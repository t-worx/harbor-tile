# Prompts and generation log

## Style preamble (verbatim on every interior still)

Luxury interior architectural photography of a coastal Palm Beach home,
full-frame camera, 24mm tilt-shift lens, verticals corrected, eye height.
Natural late-afternoon sunlight entering from the right through tall windows,
soft warm bounce, gentle falloff into cool shadow. Colour grade: warm white,
sun-bleached sand, soft sea-glass turquoise, deep shadow blue. Fine film grain,
real surface texture, honest reflections on polished stone. Photographic
realism. NOT 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT illustration, no digital glow, no plastic
sheen, no people, no text.

Exterior and yacht shots keep the light, grade, texture and negative lines
word for word and swap only the medium line (drone / yacht salon).
